# I01 plan — amendment 6 (owner ruling, 2026-10-29)

This amendment corrects the record of the order of events for amendment 4. It changes no estimand, estimator or stop-rule. `I01_plan.md` and
amendments 1 to 5 are not edited; they are cited by file name and SHA-256 in `krl.pap`, not by commit id.

## Owner ruling (2026-10-29)

1. **The order of events.** The owner approved amendment 4 in the working session's chat **before** the fiscal 2022 certification-length test was
   run. The test therefore rests on an approved amendment. Amendment 4 was committed as a draft for owner review, approved by the owner in chat,
   and only then was the test run.
2. **Amendment 5 is corrected.** Amendment 5's statements that the test was run before the owner ratified amendment 4, and that the ratification
   was given after the result was known, are **withdrawn**. They came from a misreading of the title of the amendment 4 file, not from the record.
   The rest of amendment 5 stands: the layout the owner accepts, and that the fiscal 2022 result is not a replication of the fiscal 2019 result.
3. **The title of the amendment 4 file.** `I01_plan_amendment_4.md` still reads "DRAFT for owner review" because it is the text that was
   committed for approval, and a committed plan file is never edited. The title records the state at commit, not the state at the run.
4. **What stays as it was.** Amendment 4 was written after the 15th edition's text had been read: the Review 4 pass searched it and found its
   certification-period heading before the amendment named the heading and fixed the coding rule. The fiscal 2022 test therefore stays labelled
   "not pre-specified (added after review, 2026-10-29)" and stays outside the Holm family. It is not a replication of the fiscal 2019
   certification-length result; it measures households with simplified reporting.
5. **The earlier commits.** The message of the commit that recorded amendment 5's sequence is not rewritten; this amendment is the correction of
   record. The notebook's text is corrected to the sequence in item 1.

## Unchanged

The question, the estimators, the Holm family, the primary year (fiscal 2019, amendment 3), the replications, the EITC pairing and every
stop-rule. From this point no analysis rests on a draft amendment: work stops at the draft and the owner is asked.
