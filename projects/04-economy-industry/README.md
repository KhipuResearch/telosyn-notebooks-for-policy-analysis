## Industry Structure

**Data Withheld on Purpose, and Published as Zero.**

`D09_industry_structure/D09_industry_structure.ipynb`

**What it shows**

- Distinguish disclosure suppression from sampling error and from model uncertainty, and say which analytical tools each one disables.
- Detect a sentinel that is a plausible value — a withheld cell published as `0` — and quantify the error that treating it as data produces.
- Measure the precision-versus-coverage trade-off imposed by disclosure policy across levels of industry detail.
- Establish that missingness is informative by relating it to a variable the publisher *does* disclose, and extract what a suppression flag itself tells you.

**Sources** — QCEW area 51013, 2024 Q1; QCEW area 51059, 2024 Q1; QCEW area 51600, 2024 Q1; QCEW area 51720, 2024 Q1; QCEW area 51750, 2024 Q1  
5 provenance records committed in `D09_industry_structure/provenance/`.

**Limitations**

- Suppression is a rule, not a random process, so nothing here is a sampling statement and no interval is computable. The withheld employment is bounded below by zero and above by the control total's residual, and nothing narrower is available from published data.
- Five areas, chosen to span size. They are an illustration of a gradient, not a sample. The correlation reported in section 4 describes these five places only.
- One quarter, one state's areas. Suppression patterns differ by industry mix and by state disclosure practice, neither of which is examined.

## Business Establishments

**Two Agencies, Opposite Ways to Hide the Same Thing.**

`D15_business_and_entrepreneurship/D15_business_and_entrepreneurship.ipynb`

**What it shows**

- Contrast two disclosure-limitation strategies — withholding with a flag, and omission with noise infusion — and derive what each one allows and prevents.
- Detect silent omission by comparing an industry roster against a second source, since a missing row leaves no trace in the response that contains it.
- Quantify the employment that one source reports openly and the other does not publish at all.
- Read a noise flag for what it does and does not tell you, and state the limit when the publisher documents a protection it does not let you measure.

**Sources** — County Business Patterns 2022; QCEW area 51013, 2022 Q1; QCEW area 51059, 2022 Q1; QCEW area 51720, 2022 Q1; QCEW area 51750, 2022 Q1  
8 provenance records committed in `D15_business_and_entrepreneurship/provenance/`.

**Limitations**

- Four counties in one state. They illustrate a contrast; they are not a sample, and no quantity here should be read as a national figure.
- The universes genuinely differ. CBP excludes most government, agricultural production and the self-employed without payroll; QCEW covers UI-subject employment. The private six-digit comparison narrows this and does not eliminate it, and the residual is not decomposed.
- The roster comparison cannot separate disclosure from existence. An industry in QCEW and not in CBP may have been omitted for disclosure reasons *or* may fall outside CBP's universe. The 120 industries where QCEW discloses employment are the ones where that ambiguity is smallest, and even there it is not zero.
