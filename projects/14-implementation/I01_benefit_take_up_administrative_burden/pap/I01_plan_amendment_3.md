# I01 plan — amendment 3 (owner ruling, 2026-10-29)

Committed **before the notebook is re-run on the new primary year**. It supersedes amendment 1, section C, on the question of which
year is primary, and changes nothing else. `I01_plan.md` and amendments 1 and 2 are not edited; they are cited here by file name and SHA-256
(the plan, `07683027fa91...`; amendment 1, `558aa55fe126...`; amendment 2, the digest in `krl.pap`), not by commit id.

## What was wrong

Plan section 4 says each fiscal year is joined to the State Options Report edition whose "as of" date is nearest the start of that fiscal year,
within 18 months, and that **the primary year is the latest fiscal year with a pairing**. Amendment 1, section C, named fiscal 2018 as primary and
fiscal 2016 as the replication, and left fiscal 2019 out. That **misapplied the rule**: fiscal 2019 begins on 1 October 2018, the 14th edition's
options are as of 1 October 2017, twelve months earlier and inside the 18-month limit, so fiscal 2019 pairs with the 14th edition and is the latest
paired year.

Amendment 1 was written after the approved files had been read once for layout (row counts, parse coverage and the printed rows at the top of each
table), **and the fiscal 2019 report's table had already been parsed in that read** (it gave 49 of 51 jurisdictions, from the split names the
amendment repairs). The choice of fiscal 2018 over fiscal 2019 was therefore made with the fiscal 2019 table's layout, and some of its leading rows,
already seen. No gap, feature coding or association had been computed for any year.

## Owner ruling (2026-10-29)

Follow the plan.

- **Primary year: fiscal 2019, with the 14th edition** (options as of 1 October 2017; 12 months from the start of fiscal 2019).
- **Replications, not pooled and not corrected: fiscal 2018 with the 14th edition** (0 months) **and fiscal 2016 with the 12th edition** (0 months).
- The Holm family (online application, certification length) is the two tests in the primary year, as before.
- The fiscal 2019 report's table is read by the same rules as the others (amendment 1, section A, including the name and number repairs and the
  51-of-51 coverage rule). The EITC is paired by the calendar year in which the fiscal year ends, as before: fiscal 2019 with tax year 2019.
- Fiscal 2022 is unchanged: the SNAP gap and the SNAP–EITC correlation only, because the options test is not possible there.
- The notebook states this history where it names the primary year.

## Unchanged

Everything else in `I01_plan.md` and amendments 1 and 2, including the question, the estimators, the expected directions and every stop-rule.
