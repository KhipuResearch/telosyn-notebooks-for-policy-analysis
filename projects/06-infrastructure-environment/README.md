## Environmental Economics

**When Every Number Says How It Was Made.**

`D12_environmental_economics/D12_environmental_economics.ipynb`

**What it shows**

- Read a per-record provenance flag as data about the observation rather than metadata about the file, and say what share of a published series rests on measurement.
- Choose a weighting deliberately, and test whether a weighted quality statistic is a property of the population or of one observation.
- Distinguish a directional loss from a material one, by bounding a known gap in both records and magnitude rather than assuming the sign implies the size.
- Detect a vocabulary change inside a single column, and see why the documented change year is not a clean cut in either direction.

**Sources** — BLS QCEW; EPA TRI; FBI CDE

**Limitations**

- The reporting universe is a threshold, not a sample. Nothing here supports an inference about releases from facilities below the thresholds, and the number of such facilities is not published. Absence from TRI is not evidence of absence of release.
- The numbers are self-reported and unverified by EPA. The basis code says what method the filer says they used. Nothing in the data attests that they used it.
- The basis code is categorical. No quantification of error attaches to any code, so the record shares in section 2 cannot be converted into a bound on the total.

## Commuting

**The Universe That Walked Away.**

`D14_transportation_commuting/D14_transportation_commuting.ipynb`

**What it shows**

- Read the universe before the number. Two tables in the same subject area cover different populations, and the difference is where the finding lives.
- Recognise compositional change in a mean that never touches an individual's behaviour.
- Bound what you cannot decompose. Without microdata the split cannot be identified — but it can be bracketed, and the bracket is informative.
- Check an approximation against a published exact value rather than trusting it.

**Sources** — American Community Survey (acs/acs5)  
2 provenance records committed in `D14_transportation_commuting/provenance/`.

**Limitations**

- One state, two vintages. The universe mechanism is general; the magnitudes are Virginia's and the period is one that was unusually disruptive.
- The decomposition is not identified. §2 gives a bound, not a split. Resolving it needs microdata (PUMS), which this corpus has not yet touched.
- Overlapping samples. The 2015–2019 and 2019–2023 releases share a year. Differences between them are correlated and the source publishes no margin for the difference.

## Broadband Access

**Two Ways to Publish a Number That Is Not There.**

`D16_internet_and_technology_access/D16_internet_and_technology_access.ipynb`

**What it shows**

- Test whether a published table's categories actually partition their parent, instead of inferring it from indentation, and recognise the label convention that marks the exclusive rows.
- Distinguish "broadband of any type" from fixed broadband and from cellular-only access, and quantify how far the choice of definition reorders the country.
- Compute margins of error for county proportions and compare them against the same quantity at state level, to see what changing geographic grain costs.
- Apply a coefficient-of-variation reliability screen, and then demonstrate its limits by showing what it does and does not certify.

**Sources** — American Community Survey (acs/acs5)  
2 provenance records committed in `D16_internet_and_technology_access/provenance/`.

**Limitations**

- ACS 5-year estimates are period estimates spanning 2019–2023. Broadband subscription changed materially over that window, including through pandemic-era subsidy programmes that began and ended inside it. A 5-year estimate averages over that and describes no single year.
- Subscription is not availability, and neither is speed. B28002 records what a household reports paying for. A household with no service in an area with fibre at the kerb is recorded identically to one with no infrastructure at all. Nothing here measures what is offered, at what price, or at what speed; the FCC Broadband Data Collection is the source for availability, and it is a different instrument with its own well-documented accuracy disputes.
- The rank bounds are conservative by construction. They assume only that each unit's true value lies in its published interval. They make no distributional or independence assumption, so they cannot understate the ambiguity — and a narrower simulated interval would rest on assumptions a published table does not licence.
