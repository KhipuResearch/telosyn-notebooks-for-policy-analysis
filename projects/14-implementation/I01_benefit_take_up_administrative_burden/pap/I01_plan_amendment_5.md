# I01 plan — amendment 5 (owner ruling, 2026-10-29)

This amendment records the owner's ratification of amendment 4 and the order in which things happened. It changes no estimand, estimator or
stop-rule. `I01_plan.md` and amendments 1 to 4 are not edited; they are cited by file name and SHA-256 in `krl.pap`, not by commit id.

## Owner ruling (2026-10-29)

1. **Amendment 4 is ratified.** It was committed as a draft ("draft for owner review") and is now an amendment of record.
2. **The sequence, stated plainly.** The fiscal 2022 certification-length test of amendment 4 was **run before this ruling**. The ratification
   was given **after its result was known**: Spearman rho +0.093, 95% state-bootstrap interval −0.208 to +0.380. The test is therefore
   not pre-specified in the sense that matters for inference, and stays labelled "not pre-specified (added after review, 2026-10-29)", outside
   the Holm family.
3. **What had been read before amendment 4 was written.** Amendment 4 was written after the 15th edition's text had been read: the Review 4
   pass searched it and found its certification-period heading (58 matches) before the amendment named the heading and fixed the coding rule.
4. **The layout.** The owner accepts the 15th edition's five bulleted lists as the certification period "printed once per jurisdiction" that
   amendment 4, section "Coding rule", item 1, requires. The test was not stopped for this reason.
5. **Not a replication.** The fiscal 2022 result is not a replication of the fiscal 2019 certification-length result. It measures the
   certification period of households with simplified reporting, a subset of cases.

## Unchanged

The question, the estimators, the Holm family, the primary year (fiscal 2019, amendment 3), the replications, the EITC pairing and every
stop-rule. From this point no analysis rests on a draft amendment: work stops at the draft and the owner is asked.
